3D MODEL GENERATION

From photos to a real mesh

360° photos create a viewable image spin, not 3D geometry. To create a realistic GLB/GLTF mesh, process the photos with a photogrammetry or image-to-3D service, then import that model here.

  1. Capture 24–72 overlapping photos all around the motorcycle.
  2. Keep distance, lighting and camera height consistent.
  3. Generate and export a .glb or .gltf model.
  4. Use Import 3D to inspect the actual mesh in the viewer.

This demo does not send your photos to an AI service or claim to generate a 3D model locally.
